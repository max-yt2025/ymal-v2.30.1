What We Watch For

Understanding unwanted bot activity

Artificial activity can make a stream's analytics difficult to understand. In some situations, malicious actors may intentionally send unwanted activity toward a creator.

👁️

View Botting

Artificial viewers can create unusual traffic patterns and make legitimate analytics harder to interpret.

👥

Follower Botting

Artificial followers can suddenly change follower counts without representing genuine audience growth.

⚠️

Targeted Activity

A creator may receive unwanted traffic or followers without requesting them. Documenting the event can help with an investigation.

Our Response

How YMAL responds

We focus on awareness, documentation, warnings and responsible reporting rather than retaliation.

1

Identify

Look for unusual viewer, follower or traffic patterns that may indicate unwanted activity.

2

Warn

Let the affected streamer know about suspicious activity and recommend preserving relevant evidence.

3

Document

Record timestamps, screenshots and other non-sensitive information that can help establish what happened.

4

Report

Use official platform reporting and support channels when suspicious activity is found.

5

Protect

Help the creator understand what happened without encouraging harassment, retaliation or unauthorized actions.
